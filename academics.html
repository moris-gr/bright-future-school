Academics

Quality education from Ordinary Level to Advanced Level

Our Academic Programs

We offer a broad and balanced curriculum

Ordinary Level (S.1 – S.4)

Students study a wide range of subjects including Mathematics, English, Sciences, History, Geography, Languages and vocational subjects leading to the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE).

Advanced Level (S.5 – S.6)

Students choose specialised combinations in Sciences, Arts or Business. We prepare them thoroughly for the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) and university entry.

ICT & Skills Development

Computer Studies, digital literacy, and practical skills that prepare students for modern careers and higher education.

Subject Combinations (A-Level)

Sciences

PCM, PEM, PCB, BCM and other science combinations with strong laboratory support.

Arts

HEG, HEL, HED, Literature, Divinity and related combinations.

Business

Economics, Entrepreneurship, Geography and related business combinations.

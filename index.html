Welcome to Bright Future Secondary School

Building bright futures through quality education in Uganda

Bright Future Secondary School is a co-educational day and boarding school committed to providing a stimulating learning environment that develops the intellectual, social, moral and physical abilities of every learner.

We prepare students for national examinations (UCE & UACE), higher education and responsible citizenship while instilling the values of integrity, discipline, excellence and service to the community.

Admissions

Join Bright Future Secondary School and give your child the foundation for a successful future.

How to Apply

A simple four-step process

1

Collect Forms

Visit the school or download the application form from our office. Forms are available throughout the year.

2

Submit Documents

Return the completed form with the required documents and application fee to the admissions office.

3

Entrance Assessment

Sit for a short entrance test and interview (for selected classes). Dates are communicated after submission.

4

Receive Offer

Successful applicants receive an admission letter with fee structure and reporting dates.

Required Documents

  • Completed application form
  • Copy of birth certificate or national ID
  • Two recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school report cards / academic transcripts
  • Leaving certificate from previous school (where applicable)
  • Medical form / immunisation record
  • Application fee receipt

Contact our admissions office for more information or to book a school visit.
